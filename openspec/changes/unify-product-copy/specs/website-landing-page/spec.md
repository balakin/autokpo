## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Serbian Latin product positioning

The landing page SHALL present AutoKPO in Serbian Latin at the website root as a free, open-source KPO record-keeping tool for preduzetnici paušalci in Serbia, while localized English and Russian landing pages SHALL present equivalent product positioning for flat-rate entrepreneurs in Serbia. The English landing metadata and visible product copy SHALL prefer "KPO record-keeping" and "flat-rate entrepreneurs in Serbia" over literal descriptions such as "Book of Achieved Turnover".

#### Scenario: Hero communicates product purpose

- **WHEN** a visitor reads the hero section on any localized landing page
- **THEN** the page explains that AutoKPO helps flat-rate entrepreneurs in Serbia manage KPO records and income tracking in that page's locale

#### Scenario: Free and open-source positioning is visible

- **WHEN** a visitor reviews any localized landing page
- **THEN** the page states in that page's locale that AutoKPO is free and open source

#### Scenario: Metadata uses unified product positioning

- **WHEN** a crawler reads the landing page title or meta description for any supported locale
- **THEN** the metadata describes AutoKPO as KPO record-keeping for flat-rate entrepreneurs in Serbia in that page's locale
